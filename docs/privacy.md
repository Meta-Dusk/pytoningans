# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026  
**Project Repository:** [https://github.com/Meta-Dusk/pytoningans](https://github.com/Meta-Dusk/pytoningans)  

PyToNingans is an open-source desktop idler sandbox game and modding platform developed by MetaDusk. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you provide when using our services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

PyToNingans is designed as an **offline-first application**. You do not need to provide any personal data or create an account to use the application or download local mods.

If you choose to sign in using Google OAuth or create a community creator account to publish mods:

* **Account Information**: We receive your Google account email address, display name, and avatar image URL via standard Google OAuth 2.0 (`openid`, `email`, `profile` scopes).
* **Creator Profile**: We store your creator handle, published mods, and community mod ratings.

We do **not** collect passwords (when signing in via Google OAuth), payment information, device identifiers, or tracking telemetry.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Any information collected is used strictly to:

* Authenticate your identity as a verified mod author.
* Associate community mods, modpacks, releases, and reviews with your creator handle.
* Prevent duplicate accounts and abusive or spam community uploads.

We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

We utilize the following infrastructure providers to support authentication and community mod hosting:

* **Google OAuth 2.0**: Handles user authentication. Google's privacy policy can be viewed at [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **Supabase**: Provides secure cloud database storage and authentication session tokens for published community mods. Supabase's privacy policy can be viewed at [https://supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Your profile information is retained for as long as your creator account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated published mods at any time by contacting us.
